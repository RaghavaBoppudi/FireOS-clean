# Notices

## Background wallpapers

The gradients in `assets/backgrounds/` are generated by [`wallpapers/generate.py`](wallpapers/generate.py), which
paints soft pools of colour, blurs them and adds a fine grain. They are original to this project and released under
CC0, so they carry no credit on screen. Their style is inspired by Basic Apple Guy's gradient wallpapers
(https://basicappleguy.com), but none of that site's images are used: they are copyrighted and carry no licence.
Wallpapers downloaded from a GitHub repository (see the README) are credited on screen from their file names.

## Icons

The icons in `res/drawable/ic_*.xml` are filled glyphs from [Material Symbols](https://github.com/google/material-design-icons)
by Google (`sports_esports`, `apps`, `live_tv`, `autorenew`, `settings`), converted to Android vector drawables. Material
Symbols is licensed under the [Apache License 2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0). The Wi-Fi wedge
(`ic_network_*.xml`) and the Background tile (`ic_background.xml`) are drawn for this project and released under CC0.
