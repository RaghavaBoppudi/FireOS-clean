# Notices

## Background wallpapers

The dunes in `assets/backgrounds/` are generated by [`wallpapers/generate.py`](wallpapers/generate.py), which builds a
height field of sand dunes, lights it and renders it by tracing a ray through every sub-pixel. They are original to
this project and released under CC0, so they carry no credit on screen. No photograph or other image is used or copied.
Wallpapers downloaded from a GitHub repository (see the README) are credited on screen from their file names.

## Icons

The icons in `res/drawable/ic_*.xml` are filled glyphs from [Material Symbols](https://github.com/google/material-design-icons)
by Google (`sports_esports`, `apps`, `live_tv`, `contrast`, `image`, `autorenew`, `settings`), converted to Android vector drawables. Material
Symbols is licensed under the [Apache License 2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0). The Wi-Fi wedge
(`ic_network_*.xml`), the plus (`ic_add.xml`), the pencil (`ic_edit.xml`), the dock order (`ic_dock_order.xml`) and the screensaver (`ic_screensaver.xml`) are drawn for this project and released under CC0.

## App logos

The marks in `res/drawable/logo_*.xml` come from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org) (released under CC0). The marks
themselves are trademarks of their owners (Netflix, YouTube, Apple, Spotify, Plex, Twitch, Kodi, Crunchyroll, Paramount+,
Tubi, Max and VLC) and are shown only to identify the app that each disc opens.
